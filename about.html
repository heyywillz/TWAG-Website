Our Story & Heritage

About True Worshippers A/G

Rooted in faith, grounded in community, and committed to the glory of God over 30 years of faithful service in Old Tafo-Abono, Ghana.

OUR FOUNDATION

History & Founding

True Worshippers A/G was established in 1995 at Old Tafo-Abono, Ghana, under the visionary spiritual leadership of Rev. Prof. Paul Frimpong Manso. Since inception, Rev. Elvis Kwadwo has shepherded the congregation, growing it into a vibrant community of believers committed to God’s Word.

From humble beginnings in a modest structure, our church family has grown continuously in faith, outreach, and discipleship — empowering generations to live for Christ and shine as lights in our local community and beyond.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

To preach the Gospel, equip believers, and make disciples of all nations through spirit-filled worship, prayer, and authentic Christian fellowship.

Vision

A Spirit-filled church family transforming lives and communities for the Kingdom of God across Ghana and around the world.

Values

Faith, Integrity, Uncompromising Biblical Truth, Love, Prayer, and the Supreme Authority of God’s Word in all things.
